Vayakosh Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026 · App: Vayakosh (com.vayakosh.app)

The short version. Vayakosh keeps your financial information on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. We never receive, sell or share your data, because it never leaves your device.

1. Who we are

Vayakosh is a personal expense and investment tracker published by Nandan (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at vayakosh.support@gmail.com.

2. Information the app uses

SMS messages (only if you allow it)

If you turn on automatic tracking and grant SMS permission, Vayakosh reads SMS messages on your phone to find transactions sent by banks, card issuers, UPI apps, brokers and depositories. Before Android asks for permission, the app explains this and lets you choose “Not now”.

  • Each message is checked on your phone. Only messages recognised as financial transactions are saved. All others, including personal conversations, are skipped and not stored.
  • For a recognised message, the app saves the transaction details it finds, such as amount, date, merchant or payee, and payment method. It also keeps the text of that message so you can check what was detected.
  • SMS messages and the details taken from them are never uploaded, shared or used for advertising.

Vayakosh uses the READ_SMS permission to scan your inbox and RECEIVE_SMS to detect new transaction messages while the app is running. You can use the app without granting either permission by adding transactions yourself.

Information you enter

Income, expenses, investments, holdings, SIPs, budgets, goals, categories and merchant rules that you add or edit.

App settings

Preferences such as theme, reminders and whether SMS tracking is on.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your phone, for example to log daily spending or add monthly income. These are created on the device. We do not send them from a server.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, or usage or crash analytics. The app has no sign-in and no account.

3. How the information is used

Only to provide the app’s features on your phone: recording transactions, sorting them into categories, showing your monthly cashflow, budgets and investments, and sending the reminders you turned on.

4. Where your data is stored

Everything is stored in the app’s private storage on your phone. Android isolates this storage from other apps. Vayakosh does not send your data over the internet, has no backend server, and includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

The app is set to exclude its data from Android cloud backup, so your financial records are not copied to Google Drive or any other backup service.

5. Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers, lenders or other third parties. We cannot access your data at all.

6. How long data is kept

DataKept for
Individual spending and income entriesThe current calendar month. At the start of a new month, earlier months are rolled up into monthly totals and the individual entries are deleted.
Investment contributionsAbout 6 months, so investment trends can be shown.
Monthly totals, holdings, SIPs, budgets, goals, rules and settingsUntil you delete them or uninstall the app.
SMS messages that are not transactionsNever stored.

7. Your choices and control

  • Turn off SMS tracking at any time in the app under Profile.
  • Revoke permissions in Android Settings → Apps → Vayakosh → Permissions.
  • Delete transactions in the app, one at a time or with Profile → Clear all expenses.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app, or with Android Settings → Apps → Vayakosh → Storage → Clear storage. Because no copy exists anywhere else, this permanently removes all of your data.

Because we hold none of your data, we have nothing to provide, correct or delete on our side. You have full control on your device. You can still contact us with any privacy question, including about your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or other laws that apply to you.

8. Security

Your data is protected by Android’s app sandbox and your device’s own security. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so we recommend a screen lock.

9. Children

Vayakosh is a personal finance tool intended for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly process children’s information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released. If a change would ever involve your data leaving your phone, the app will ask for your consent first.
